How to link this display

  1. On your phone or computer, sign in to FieldLink Admin and open Kiosk.
  2. Click 🔗 Link kiosk next to the key for this display. No key yet? + Generate Key first.
  3. Type the code shown on the left and confirm.
  4. The map appears here within a few seconds.

Codes change every 15 minutes on their own. Only Super Admins can link displays.
